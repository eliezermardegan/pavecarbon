03 / CARBONO & MRV

Evidência primeiro. Alegações depois.

Carbon performance depends on system boundaries, methodology and evidence. We distinguish operational readiness from formal verification and certification.

MRV-READY

Structured evidence

Lot IDs, mass balance, inputs, production records, installation evidence and project documentation can be structured from origin.

VERIFIED

Independent verification

Used only where data or claims have actually been verified by an appropriate entity within a defined scope.

CERTIFIED / CREDITS

Formal programme outcome

Certification or credit issuance is not assumed. Methodology, eligibility, verification and programme requirements must first be established.
